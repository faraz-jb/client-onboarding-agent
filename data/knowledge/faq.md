# AI Invention — Frequently Asked Questions

## What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer and card. Setup fees are paid up front; the monthly fee starts
after launch.

## What is your refund policy?

7-day money-back guarantee, valid before launch. If you are not happy with the
work in the first 7 days and the system has not gone live yet, you get your
money back.

## What are your support hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm.

## How do revisions work?

Two free revision rounds are included in every project. A round means you send
your consolidated feedback, we apply it, and you review again. Changes beyond
those two rounds, or changes that fall outside the confirmed scope, are quoted
separately.

## Who owns the data?

The client owns all data. Everything the system collects — leads, chat
transcripts, workflow records — is yours. You can export it at any time, and if
you leave, it goes with you.

## Can you integrate with the tools we already use?

Yes. We integrate with CRMs, spreadsheets, and email as standard, and with most
other tools that expose an API. Bring your stack to the discovery call and we
will confirm what connects before you commit to anything.

## What happens after launch?

Ongoing support at $49/mo covers maintenance and minor updates. We monitor the
system, fix what breaks, and keep it running. New features are quoted separately.
