# AI Invention — Services

## AI Chatbots

Conversational agents deployed where your customers already are: WhatsApp,
Telegram, or embedded on your website. Every chatbot does lead capture —
it collects the visitor's name, contact, and what they need, then hands that
straight into your pipeline instead of leaving it in a chat log. Runs 24/7,
so enquiries that arrive at 2am are answered and captured, not lost.

## AI Automation Workflows

Business process automation: we take a repeated manual process and run it
end to end without a person in the loop. Includes the integrations needed to
connect the process to the tools you already use, so the automation reads and
writes real data rather than living in its own silo.

## Website Design

Custom websites built on Next.js. Dark luxury visual style, mobile-first, with
SEO built in from the start — schema markup, Open Graph tags, and a sitemap,
not bolted on afterwards. Fast by default.

## AI Agents for Specific Roles

Dedicated agents scoped to one job rather than a generic assistant:

- Receptionist agent — greets, qualifies, and books.
- Sales agent — follows up on leads and moves them through the pipeline.
- Support agent — answers customer questions and escalates what it cannot handle.
